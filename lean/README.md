# lean

Two Lean 4 libraries, one lake project, no Mathlib.

| library | proves | prose |
|---|---|---|
| `Sel15` | selection `15` has a fixed, finite set of circuits (the combinatorial step, twice by two traversals) | [`docs/CIRCUITS_15.md`](../docs/CIRCUITS_15.md) §"The Lean development" |
| `FASS` | the combinatorial core of the FASS theorem: the closure checks C1–C7 on the substitution tables, and the inductions that carry them to every level | [`docs/FASS_THEOREM.md`](../docs/FASS_THEOREM.md) §11 |

## Building

```
cd lean && lake build
```

The toolchain is pinned by `lean-toolchain` (`leanprover/lean4:v4.15.0`); `elan`
installs it on first use. `Sel15` builds in under a minute. `FASS` takes
about 13 minutes per configuration file (26 in all) because the kernel
re-runs every computation, and each configuration file needs about 9 GB of
memory at its peak;
`FASS/Spectre.lean` imports `FASS/Hex.lean` only so that lake builds the two
one after the other rather than side by side. There is no `sorry` anywhere.

## FASS

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `FASS/Core.lean` | the table types; the algebra of direction maps `x ↦ s·x + c` mod 12; the substitution step on words and on leaf labels; word claims, their expansion, and `claims_all_levels` (closure + truth at level 1 ⟹ every level); the executable checks C1, C3 (with SameCorner), C4, C5, C6, C7; the routing operator `Fstep` on (dot counts, matchings + circuit counts); the periodicity lemma `iterate_period_all` and its instances `routing_all_levels`, `angles_all_levels`, `dotCounts_all_levels` |
| `FASS/DataHex.lean`, `FASS/DataSpectre.lean` | generated by `web/fass-theorem/06-isabelle-data.ts <config> lean`: the tables of `01-tables.ts`, the level-1 words and labels, the level-1 corners, the dot indicators of the configuration, and the level-1 routing states read from exact geometry |
| `FASS/Hex.lean`, `FASS/Spectre.lean` | configurations (A) `hex/128/010100000` and (B) `spectre/1278/0101000000`: the closed computations on the data, and the all-levels theorems obtained from them |

### What the theorems say

Each configuration file ends with these statements, all of them about every
level `k`:

* `words_all_levels`, `labels_all_levels`, `glued_pairs_all_levels` — every
  glued pair of child edges carries, at every level, the same direction word
  read backwards and negated, and `+c.m` against `-c.m` on the leaf labels
  (C2). The base case is the level-1 data; the step is `holds_step`, the
  algebra of §3 written out; `closed` is the finite check that the claim set is
  closed under one substitution step.
* `angles_all_levels` — the corner-angle conditions (C5) at every level, from
  the period-2 orbit of the first/last directions of the meta-edges.
* `routing_all_levels` — zero circuits in every type and a single Psi arc at
  every level, from the period-2 orbit of the routing operator started at the
  level-1 states.
* `dot_counts_all_levels` — the boundary dot counts of the nine types are the
  same at every level.
* `c1`, `ct`, `c3`, `c3_level1`, `c4`, `c6`, `buried_0050`, `not_buried_005` —
  the level-free checks C1, C3, C4, C6, C7, run on the tables.

### Trust

The general lemmas (`holds_step`, `claims_all_levels`, `labclaims_all_levels`,
`iterate_period_all`, `routing_all_levels`, `angles_all_levels`,
`dotCounts_all_levels`) are ordinary tactic proofs. The closed computations on
the data are discharged by `decide +kernel`: the Lean kernel itself reduces the
decision procedure, so nothing is trusted beyond the kernel, and `#print axioms`
on every configuration theorem reports only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and
`Quot.sound` (no `Lean.ofReduceBool`, i.e. no `native_decide` and no trust in
the compiler). Every one of those computations is also carried out,
independently, by the TypeScript scripts of `web/fass-theorem/` and by
`isabelle/shadow.py`.

Two computations are too large for one kernel run and are split: the SameCorner
relation is computed once per configuration (`sc_eq : sameCorner T = scLit`,
about three minutes) and everything else in C3 runs on the literal; the seeds
transferred into Gamma are computed per source type (`tf_1` … `tf_8`) and
concatenated (`ts_eq`). Both literals live in `Core.lean` because the two
families share them. The per-type C3 checks, and every other check, then take
seconds each.

### What is not formalised

Everything that is geometry rather than combinatorics: that the tables describe
the actual level-1 and level-2 patches (`01-tables.ts`, exact arithmetic), the
validity of those patches (winding number, Lemma 1.1), the covering-space
argument of `docs/FASS_THEOREM.md` §4, and the vertex stars and chord crossings
of §6. Those are the inputs of this development, exactly as V0b and V6 are the
inputs of `Sel15`.

### Regenerating the data

```
cd web
npx --yes tsx fass-theorem/06-isabelle-data.ts hex128 lean > ../lean/FASS/DataHex.lean
npx --yes tsx fass-theorem/06-isabelle-data.ts spectre1278 lean > ../lean/FASS/DataSpectre.lean
```

The same script without `lean` emits the Isabelle data theories of
[`isabelle/`](../isabelle/), which is the same development in Isabelle/HOL.
